Artwork fonts
-------------
art/*.png use Inter (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
The app's embedded font (App/FontAtlas.h) and the app screenshot use DejaVu Sans
(Bitstream Vera licence). Both licences allow embedding in software and artwork.

Aurora .asset format
--------------------
tools/aurora_asset.py is an independent implementation of the format as
documented by the Aurora community (jrobiche/xbox360-aurora-developer-documentation,
Aurora Asset Editor). Its headers were checked byte-for-byte against .asset files
known to display on hardware (NetDisc360's FastFTP360 art, MIT licence).
